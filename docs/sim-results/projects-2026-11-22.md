# Gold and Science projects for every empire: all-Hard balance check (2026-11-22)

Every empire can now run a Gold project (25% of a city's Production) or a Science project (10%); Germany keeps 50% and 25%. With the final branch, no faction's win rate moved beyond noise against master.

Setup: `legatum-sim`, all-Hard scripted AI, `--rotate-factions`, 4 threads. Pool 1 is Egypt, Switzerland, India, Byzantium, Germany, Celts, Mongols, Sparta (seeds 7000+); pool 2 is Egypt, Athens, Aztecs, Rome, Switzerland, Germany, Mongols, Sparta (seeds 9000+). Each arm: 100 games per pool at seed offsets 0 and 100, 400 games in all, same seeds for every arm.

Average game length 258 turns on master and 259 on the branch; 3 stalled games out of 400 in each.

The first branch build cut AI Egypt to 6% at every rate tried (15/8, 25/10, 33/15), because a new AI filter on Gold-seeking swaps also blocked Egypt's Grow. Hiding Gold and Science projects from Egypt changed nothing; letting the swaps keep Grow restored Egypt (27 of 100 against master's 29 on the same seeds). Those rate arms were run before the fix, so they say nothing about the rates; 25/10 stands on the designer's pick. Rome's near-zero win rate is unchanged and is the AI's known weakness with Rome.
