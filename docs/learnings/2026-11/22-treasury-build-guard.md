## 2026-11-22: Hard trained troops at a funding front until the treasury ran dry (`treasury_build_guard`)

- **The bug.** A city marked as a funding front (threatened, or near a war front) was exempt from pausing unfunded production (`funding.rs`, `production.rs`), so a broke Hard seat kept finishing upkeep-paying troops, ran out of Gold and lost units to bankruptcy turn after turn. The final budget also left out troops completing this turn.
- **Fix: gene `treasury_build_guard` (Hard, default 1).** Even at a funding front, an upkeep-paying build is paused when its added upkeep would make the current turn insolvent, and completing troops count in the final budget. The emergency defence exception stays when the army is payable.
- **Measured (rules 50, 200 all-Hard games, seeds 20001-20200).** Units lost to an empty treasury 519 -> 334; India carries most of it (261 -> 112). Aztec treasury losses rose slightly (+13), and Athens, Rome and Switzerland ordered a few more disbands. Duel (one gene seat vs five without, 430 seeds): VP share at the end +0.07 pp [-0.05, +0.19], neutral. Rusher gate: 35.0% -> 34.5% capture by T60, neutral.
- **Left over.** About a quarter of ordered disbands come from production starting troops at a 10-turn runway while the army trim cuts below 4 turns; a shared runway floor (`shared_runway_floor`) is a separate candidate.
- **It overrides India's front exceptions when the turn cannot pay.** `india_emergency_defense` and `india_prewar_guard` let a threatened Indian city start a troop despite a short runway; with the guard on, a troop that makes the current turn insolvent is refused there too (it would be disbanded for upkeep at once). Two older India tests asserted the exception at 0 Gold and a negative income, so they now turn the guard off for that assertion and check the refusal with it on. Flipping a gene's default needs the whole AI test group, not only the gene's own tests.
