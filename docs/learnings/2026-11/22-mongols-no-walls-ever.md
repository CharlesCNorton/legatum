## 2026-11-22: Mongols never have Walls (rules 55)

- **Why the AI Mongols win so often (24.7% of 2,000 six-seat all-Hard games on rules 51) when Sam finds them fragile:** +1 move is a strength the AI uses with no plan (the 2026-11-05 ablation: −4.3 pp when off), while their drawback, open cities, only costs what rivals make it cost, and Hard AIs rarely march on a city (hill-climb traces). Against an AI field the drawback measured only +4.8 pp; a human attacker punishes it far harder. A faction's AI-only win share partly measures how well the *other* AIs exploit its drawback, so read it beside human games.
- **One family test, not a list of kinds:** `data::is_walls_line` (any building whose `building_family` is Walls) plus `data::never_walls(off, faction)` replace the old `Walls | Castle | StarFort` matches, so wall levels added later are covered without touching the Mongol rule. The exclusion now sits in `available_to_faction_rules`, so the tech card's unlock list leaves Walls out for the Mongols; `research::locked_note` lost its only use and was removed (the field stays, always None).
- **`MONGOL_WALLS_ERA` was doing two jobs** (Walls and Siege). It is now `MONGOL_SIEGE_ERA`, Siege only; the app reads `RULES.mongolSiegeEra`.
- **Captured walls come down** in `rules/battle.rs::capture_city` (Sam chose it). The sim-only upgrade of a rules-54 state leaves Walls already standing in a Mongol city; only new captures and builds follow the rule.
