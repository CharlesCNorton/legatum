## 2026-11-23: Hard reserve and Settler oscillation guards

- **Cause.** Peace-time supported defence could reverse a routed field move on consecutive turns, including when peaceful Scouts could not capture a healthy guarded city. A reserve could also be pulled between nearby cities, and a Settler could step out into a visible capture threat without a reachable escort.
- **Decision.** Enable `peace_scout_stage_guard`, `reserve_staging_stable`, `peace_stage_guard_all` at value 1, and `settler_stepout_check` for Hard. The guards retain the existing timed-out Settler escape. A pending garrison departure makes a Peace guard ineligible, so the ordinary staging gate decides whether to recall it. The code reads only the seat's view and changes no core rule or save format.
- **Evidence before landing.** On the rules-51 candidate, the all-on arm cut 50-game A-B-A-B movement episodes from 2,609 to 1,293. A 430-seed duel estimated end VP share +0.40 percentage points [−0.08, +0.87]; the 24-cell held-out mean rose 2.17 points. The 220-seed rusher gate was neutral within its uncertainty interval, and the opening league stalled in 0 of 20 games. These measurements predate both the rules-57 port and the departing-garrison correction, so they do not quantify the final tree's strength.
- **Safety finding.** The earlier Peace guard could suppress `stage_supported_defence`, which was also the code that canceled a departing garrison's move. In that case the safety read counted a defender the orders would remove. The landing treats the departure as a reason not to suppress the ordinary staging gate, and adds a constructed-state regression for Scout, all-Peace and forecast-gated guards. The Germany T12 escort replay also checks that an existing routed escort is not canceled by a new direct garrison hold. A fresh strength screen on rules 55 remains useful.
