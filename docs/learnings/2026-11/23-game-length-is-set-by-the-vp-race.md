## 2026-11-23: Game length is set by the VP race, not by the tech pace

- **Cause.** Sam's games ended at T141 to T168 with him in the World War era and his rivals in Industrial, so he rarely saw the late units. His era entries came every 22 to 25 turns, the same as the AI leaders in sims: research does not accelerate. What ends the game is conquest: 10 to 15 cities fell in his last 40 turns, and a rival capital with a world wonder is 3 VP to the taker.
- **Evidence.** 256 six-empire all-Hard games per arm (`crates/sim/examples/escalation_curve.rs`, `tools/sim/escalation_report.py`, report in `docs/sim-results/game-length/README.md`). Every technology 20% cheaper (`tech-cost-percent=80`): each World Age about 15 turns sooner, median game 208 to 202 turns, Future-era winners 44% to 63%. VP target 12: median 234, the climb from 5 VP to the win 46 to 66 turns. Tech pace moves the era a game ends in; the VP target moves how long it runs.
- **What changed.** No rule (Sam, 2026-11-23: wait for the Hard AI and Walls levels work first). The measuring tool and the arms are in `docs/sim-results/game-length/README.md`; the recommended lever if pacing still needs it is every tech 20% cheaper plus a VP target of 12.
- **Rule for later.** To lengthen games, change the VP clock (target, what captures are worth, how well cities hold), not tech costs; to show more eras, change tech costs. A 10th era added at the end of the tree does nothing for games that end in the World War era. All-AI sims do not reproduce a human steamroll: check pacing against the designer's own games.
