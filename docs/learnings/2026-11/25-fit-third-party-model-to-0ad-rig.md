## 2026-11-25: Fitting a posed third-party model onto the 0 A.D. rig

- Cause: the premium look needs a better model than 0 A.D.'s, on the approved 0 A.D. motion. Sketchfab hoplites are static sculpts in action poses.
- What works (`tools/art/anim3d/kit/fit_source_to_0ad.py`, `kit/FIT.md`): solid-voxel geodesic tips (farthest-point growth) find head, hands and feet; joints at the 0 A.D. bone lengths from each tip; per-bone segment maps blended by slerp about the shared joint; displacement relaxed at tears; 0 A.D. weights by chain-masked Data Transfer; clips copied byte for byte from the approved GLB.
- Dead ends: ordering tips by distance from the root (thick limbs grow parallel paths, so one hand gives several tips); surface-only voxels (the back and chest sprout tips); taking the head as the tip nearest the root (the 0 A.D. hanging hands touch the flared tunic and shortcut); calibrating joint arc lengths by projecting 0 A.D. joints onto its own geodesic paths (same shortcut; use summed bone lengths); linear blending of two maps 150 degrees apart (collapses the shoulder); projecting limb vertices onto the voxel centre line (it bends into the body at the root joint and the projection jumps, tearing the thigh top).
- Contract notes: a fitted spear, shield or cloak larger than 0 A.D.'s sinks into the floor in walk/death; the fit scales the spear to 0 A.D. length, caps the shield at 1.1x and cuts the cloak to the 0 A.D. hem. `validate_glb.py` now accepts a credit that contains the 0 A.D. credit and needs cloak trim only when the unit has a cloak.
- Evidence: proof on a CC0 MPFB lunge (mesh only): contract valid, Khronos 0/0, review files `review/3d-v2/spearman-fit-test-*`.
