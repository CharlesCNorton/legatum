# Pikeman round 1 sources

- Pikeman painting: https://pub-fb5c281dd12d4ea4bbd38d657cdeb907.r2.dev/app/src/assets/units/pikeman-source.png — original project CC BY 4.0. Full source registered to the committed 256 token, projected with frozen UVs on actual full-depth articulated geometry.
- MPFB 2.0.18: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/releases/tag/v2.0.18 — GPL-3.0-or-later software; CC0 body, morphs and game_engine weights. Reuses the isolated installation.
- Quaternius Universal Animation Library Standard, 2025-06-10: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html — CC0-1.0, local LICENSE retained. Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Hit_Chest, Death01. Immutable mirror: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library/e24c23cf2a1323488a3faa226ea7ea21f644b73e/glTF/AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.gltf and adjacent `.bin`. Reference/rest matrices and bone roll compensated with Blender world-space COPY_ROTATION. Two-handed carrying constraints layer over captured upper-body motion; feet are never rotated after retargeting. The weapon-specific thrust is original.
- Unseen linen and steel fills: existing mounted-family original generated artwork, 2026-11-22, project CC BY 4.0, `assets/horseman/painted-fills.png` and derived `fill-linen.png` / `fill-bronze.png`. Built-in imagegen, no public URL. Prompt: four-quadrant painted coat/linen/bronze/hair texture sheet for premium historical miniatures, subtle brushwork, no objects/text/insignia. Bronze brushwork is desaturated and tinted within the steel shader, mixed with physical metal plus actual AO/Pointiness bakes.
- Blender 5.3.0: https://www.blender.org/ — GPL software. Pillow 13.0.0: https://pypi.org/project/pillow/13.0.0/ — MIT-CMU. Existing FFmpeg.
- Original kit and pipeline scripts: MIT; rendered art CC BY 4.0. Pipeline helpers reviewed using `git show astra/unit-anim-3d-barbarian-pikeman` (1d221b56); infantry standard read from `astra/unit-anim-3d-v2` (d1d60f6f). Rest-space retarget helper is an isolated snapshot of the trench-infantry lane, copied after reading it. No paid/account-gated assets, Mixamo, upload or service.

Input hashes are recorded in `assets.lock.json`; all assets, .blend files, bakes and 512/256 masters stay under `~/LegatumRuns/anim-pilot/` outside git.
