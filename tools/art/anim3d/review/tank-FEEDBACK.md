- (lead, crash fix 2026-11-22) Blender crashes reached Sam's screen as macOS "Blender quit unexpectedly" dialogs. Causes: (1) Cycles turning tiny/zero-scaled hidden smoke meshes into volume grids (OpenVDB ScaleMap exception, SIGABRT); (2) earlier, a Blender launched without -b touching the macOS UI from the sandbox. Rules now: never scale an object to 0 or near 0 to hide it; hide effects with keyed hide_render/visibility, and give volume/smoke meshes a sane size. Always run Blender with -b. Every `blender` call on your PATH now goes through a machine-wide gate: ONE Blender process on the whole Mac at a time, 3 threads, auto-retry on crash. Don't call /Applications/Blender.app directly. Expect to wait for the lock; keep renders short.
